Elecrow 5.0" ESP32-S3: Theremin Hardware Architecture

This diagram illustrates the final optimized hardware topology for the Theremin, centered around the Elecrow CrowPanel 5.0". To bypass any analog pin limitations on the screen, an ADS1115 I2C ADC is used to read all potentiometers cleanly.

The Power of the I2C Bus: Notice how the two ToF sensors and the ADC expander all share the exact same 4-wire I2C port from the Elecrow board. This massively reduces wiring complexity inside the instrument chassis.

Module Connects to... Signal Type Notes
VL53L5CX (Left) Elecrow I2C Port + 2x GPIO I2C + Digital Out + Digital In Needs a dedicated GPIO to its `LPn` / `RST` pin for boot sequence, and another GPIO for the `INT` (Data Ready) pin.
VL53L5CX (Right) Elecrow I2C Port + 2x GPIO I2C + Digital Out + Digital In Needs a dedicated GPIO to its `LPn` / `RST` pin for boot sequence, and another GPIO for the `INT` (Data Ready) pin.
ADS1115 (ADC) Elecrow I2C Port I2C Reads the 4 potentiometers and converts them to digital data for the ESP32. Set ADDR pin to GND (address 0x48).
Potentiometers (x4) ADS1115 (A0 to A3) Analog Voltage Outer pins to 3.3V and GND. Center wiper pin to the ADS1115 analog inputs.
Rotary Encoder Elecrow GPIO Headers Digital In (x3) Uses 3 standard digital pins (CLK, DT for rotation, SW for the click).
